## The same operation, after the record has aged out

A disaster-recovery replay seven days later, with 1 record(s)
evicted by the retention window. The contract now has nothing to say,
and the layers beneath it are the only thing standing between a replay
and a second effect.

| layer | arrivals stopped |
|---|---:|
| business-uniqueness | 1 |
| did-real-work | 1 |
| effect-ledger | 1 |

Arrivals 3, admitted 2, **effects 1**.

This is the case that decides whether an idempotency key is a design or
a decoration. Every de-duplication window is finite, so every system
eventually runs this scenario -- usually during a recovery, which is the
worst moment to discover the answer.
